# reference/

**A frozen snapshot, not a mirror.** These are the two skill files that actually ran on my
machine on 29 September 2026, copied verbatim and then left alone. Nothing syncs them any
more, and they will drift from whatever I'm running by the time you read this. That is
fine — they're here to be raided, not followed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`morning.md`](morning.md) | The 6am brief at full size. ~2,400 lines, a year of corrections deep. |
| [`evening.md`](evening.md) | The 5pm check-in — reads the same day back and corrects the morning's page where the day disagreed with it. |

**Read [`../SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md) first.** It is the same method with the scar tissue
removed, and it is the one you should hand to your agent. Come here when you want to see
what a specific rule looks like after it has been argued with fifty times.

## Why these are long and yours should not be

`morning.md` is long because it is honest about what went wrong, not because it is
thorough. Almost every prohibition in it exists because something specific broke once: a
date reported from the wrong timezone, a medical appointment put on the wrong Friday three
days running, a source that silently returned nothing for a fortnight while the digest
reported it as a quiet week.

It also shows the failure mode to avoid. It reached 124KB and roughly 400 prohibitions by
only ever being appended to, and the output went flat — correct, sourced, and completely
lifeless. The fix was cutting, and the rule that came out of it is at the top of the file:
**if an edit makes the file longer, look for what it supersedes.**

Start at the length of `../SKILL.md`. Let yours grow only where your own days push on it.
